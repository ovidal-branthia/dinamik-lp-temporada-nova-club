We've been working for you for months

The improvements Dinami-K is rolling out this season to take better care of every athlete

Dear families: over the last few months we have been working so that Dinami-K can take a new step forward.

In the 2026/27 season we are introducing new ways of supporting athletes and their families, expanding technical-development and specialisation opportunities, improving sporting follow-up, organising communication and making the competition experience easier.

We are doing it across three complementary pillars: a sports structure with more opportunities to progress, a closer, more structured relationship with the club and a new technology platform that will centralise information and make day-to-day life simpler.

We are not just presenting new tools. We are presenting a clearer, closer and more complete way of supporting each athlete's development.

01What we are strengthening this season

During the 2026/27 season Dinami-K is taking a step forward in four key areas of sporting development and the family experience.

Area 1
Development & technical work

To broaden the opportunities for specific work and support each athlete according to their age, development, interests and goals.

Area 2
Athlete follow-up

So every family can better understand their development, the work done and their next goals.

Area 3
Communication & organisation

So information is centralised, clearer and arrives at the right time.

Area 4
Competition experience

To make call-ups, registration, preparation and support easier for each athlete.

One same goal

Everything in this document shares one goal: to offer a clearer, closer and more complete experience to athletes and their families.

02How we do it: three pillars

We are strengthening, in a coordinated way, the sports model, the support we offer as a club and the technology tool that will let us manage it in an orderly, sustainable way.

Pillar 1
Sports model

More technical-development, specialisation and progression opportunities suited to each stage of the athlete.

Pillar 2
Club support

Meeting points to inform, answer questions, explain each category's work and share the athlete's development.

Pillar 3
New platform: Athria

A digital system that centralises communication, follow-up, competitions and day-to-day organisation.

The idea

More opportunities to progress, more support and all the information in one place.

03More opportunities to grow in the sport

At Dinami-K technical work is already done in depth in every category of the school, tailored to each age and stage. This season we wanted to go further and expand the options for specific work so every athlete can discover new events, deepen their technique and progress when they are ready.

Technical development from Sub10

We add extra sessions to go deeper into the technical work of jumps, sprints, hurdles and throws, beyond each category's base training.

A progressive model

More to discover. More specific work to progress.

Technical work is already done in depth in every category; these areas are an additional option that the technical team sets according to age, goals and places. Times and conditions in the schedule document and the new fee structure.

04We're with you all year

We set up various meeting points with families to explain how the club works, share each category's work and make the athlete's development visible.

  1. At the start of the activity

    Welcome meeting & kit

    We'll welcome families and give you a welcome kit with the essential information to understand how the club works from the very first moment.

    The kit will include, in brief
    • How the club works overall
    • Calendar, groups and schedules
    • People in charge and contacts
    • Communication model and channels
    • Getting started with Athria
    • How competitions work, basics
    • Kit and gear needed
    • Licences, registrations and payments
    • Basic rules for running and getting along
  2. Once the groups are running

    Meeting by category

    The technical management will explain to families the goals, working methodology and sporting approach of each category. Support adapts to each stage: in early training we focus on guiding families and understanding competitions; in the higher categories, on technical follow-up, sporting goals and the athlete's growing independence.

  3. Mid-season

    Progress update

    Each family will receive a structured update on the athlete's progress: work done, development observed and goals for the second half of the year. When needed, it can be complemented with an individual meeting with the technical team.

  4. Before competitions

    Getting ready for competitions

    We'll set aside a specific space to explain how competitions work and how the club supports athletes, with special attention to families going through this for the first time.

    • How the athlete's participation is planned
    • How a call-up is received
    • How to confirm participation on Athria
    • What information the family will find
    • How technical support is organised
    • What to know before competition day

What's going best

Èric has an excellent attitude with teammates and very good stamina. When an activity grabs him, he gets fully involved and carries the group along.

What we're working on

He finds it hard to get into some new activities and sometimes prefers to watch before trying. We support him to take part without fear of mistakes; it's not disinterest, it's caution.

How you can help from home

No pressure: ask him which activity he enjoyed most this week and reinforce that making mistakes is part of learning. It helps a lot when he arrives keen to try.

06How you will notice it day to day

These improvements are designed to broaden each athlete's opportunities to progress, simplify the relationship with the club and make their sporting support more visible.

All the information in one place

You'll know where to find every notice, call-up, document or activity.

More clarity about progress

You'll better understand what your child is working on and their next goals.

Competitions easier to follow

You'll get call-ups in order and can confirm participation from your phone.

Set times to talk

You'll have structured spaces to learn about the category's work and share progress.

Fewer scattered messages

The main communication will be centralised and it'll be easier to find what matters.

More support for newcomers

New families will understand sooner how the club works and what a competition is like.

The result

Fewer doubts. More clarity. More support.
